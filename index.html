Featured Athlete: Connor Sullivan

Connor Sullivan is an accomplished student athlete who transitioned from a standout career at Los Gatos High School (CA) to playing collegiate water polo at Pomona College.

During his high school career, Connor was a key player for the Los Gatos Varsity Water Polo team, earning distinctions such as **SCVAL League MVP** and **Academic All-American**. He was an influential force, helping lead his team to their first CIF-CCS Open Division tournament berth. He was noted for his strong outside shot and ambidextrous play as a driver/utility player.

Now competing at the collegiate level with the Pomona-Pitzer Sagehens, Connor is listed as a Sophomore (for the 2025 season) and is a valuable addition to the Men's Water Polo roster. His dedication to both academics and athletics embodies the spirit of a Pomona College student athlete.
